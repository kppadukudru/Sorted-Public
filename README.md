# Sorted

**Stuck on what to do? Let's get you Sorted.**

Sorted is an Android app for the oldest argument of the evening: what to watch. Instead of scrolling through streaming catalogues until the mood has passed, you swipe through a short deck of films, and the first one you settle on becomes the choice. It works for a couple deciding together on two phones, and for anyone choosing alone.

Sorted is built by [Product Zoned](https://productzoned.com). This repository holds the product documentation. The application code is kept private.

---

## How it works

**With a partner.** One of you starts a session and shares a six-letter code, and the other joins with it. You both swipe through the same films, and the moment you both like one, it's a match.

**On your own, with a movie buddy.** Choose one of four characters to curate your deck, each with a distinct taste. Noir keeps faith with the classics. Dude wants something light and entertaining. Dame looks for substance over spectacle. Jaunty is drawn to warm, whimsical, feel-good stories.

**On your own, entirely.** In *I am my own best company*, the first film you swipe right on is yours, and the evening is settled.

Along the way, Sorted keeps the choices honest. Broad searches reach past the obvious blockbusters to well-rated films you may never have heard of. Films you've already seen can be set aside with a single tap, and they won't return. Everything is filtered to the major streaming services available where you live, so you only ever match on something you can actually watch.

## Privacy by design

Sorted asks for no account and carries no advertising, analytics, or tracking. Your watchlist and your list of films already seen stay on your own phone. When you match with a partner, the app holds your swipes only for as long as it takes to find a film you both like, and then lets them go.

The full privacy policy is available at https://sorted-privacy-shield.lovable.app.
